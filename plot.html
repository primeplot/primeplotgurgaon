Overview

What Is a Plot or Land?

A plot (or land) is a legally defined, measured piece of ground with fixed boundaries that you can purchase, hold, or build on. In Gurgaon, plots are typically measured in Gaj (1 Gaj = 1 square yard = 9 sq ft), and range from compact 50 Gaj starter plots to large 500+ Gaj farmhouse-style parcels — each with its own title deed, registry, and legal ownership record.
