1. Why This Page Exists

Real estate is unfortunately a common target for impersonation scams — fraudsters sometimes pose as agents from genuine companies to collect illegal payments. This page helps you verify that you are dealing with the real Prime Plot Gurgaon team before making any decision or payment.

2. Signs of a Genuine Prime Plot Gurgaon Communication

  • Official calls/WhatsApp only from +91-9667134627.
  • Official email only from an address ending in @primeplotgurgaon.com.
  • We never ask for any booking amount before you've completed a physical, in-person site visit.
  • Our legal team is present or available during document verification — never rushed or pressured.

3. Red Flags to Watch For

  • Anyone demanding urgent payment "to hold the plot" without a site visit.
  • Deals that sound significantly cheaper than everything else in the same sector.
  • Requests to pay into a personal bank account instead of an official company account.
  • Pressure tactics like "only valid for the next hour" or refusal to share documents in advance.
  • Communication only through personal WhatsApp numbers not listed on our official website.

4. What We Will Never Ask

  • We will never ask for payment before a completed site visit.
  • We will never ask for your bank OTP, net-banking password, or card PIN.
  • We will never ask you to pay into a personal individual's account instead of a verified company account.

5. How to Verify Before Paying

Before making any payment, always call us directly at +91-9667134627 or email booking@primeplotgurgaon.com to confirm that the person you are dealing with is officially representing Prime Plot Gurgaon, and that the bank account details match our official records.

6. Report Suspicious Activity

If you believe someone is impersonating Prime Plot Gurgaon or attempting to defraud you using our name, please contact us immediately at +91-9667134627, and also report the matter to your local police cyber-crime cell.

⚠️ Note: If in doubt, always pause and verify directly with us before making any payment. Your safety and trust matter more to us than any single transaction.
