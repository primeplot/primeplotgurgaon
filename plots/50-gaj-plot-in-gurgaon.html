Overview

What Is a 50 Gaj Plot?

1 Gaj equals 1 square yard, so a 50 Gaj plot measures roughly 450 sq ft — commonly a 25×18 ft or similar dimension layout. It's one of the most popular entry-level plot sizes in Gurgaon, especially for buyers building a compact 2–3 storey home or investors looking for a lower entry price with strong appreciation potential.

50 Gaj plots are widely available across newly developing sectors and townships in Gurgaon, where infrastructure like roads, water, and electricity is being laid out fresh — often at a lower price point than established sectors.
